Where the Shop Analytics Live

The one-page executive answer to "where do I go to see that, and what do I do from this place?" Each surface: WHERE, IS THIS THE PLACE, WHAT TO DO.

1The department dashboard the main answer

WhereDashboard (local preview; public URL pending). Reference: tiktok-daily-report.vercel.app
Is this the placeYes. Videos posted by products, revenue per video, views per video, revenue per view, organic vs GMV Max split, distribution of the top 10. Built to your exact spec, refreshed every morning from Cruva.
What to do
  1. GMV tab: week over week. Green or red, and how red. That is the pacing.
  2. Financials tab: net after ads, commissions, refunds. ROAS under 1.1 means ads eat the top line.
  3. Products tab: which product carries the week (the bidet is 66% of GMV). A sampling slowdown hits there first.
  4. Creative tab: top videos and hooks. High views, low revenue per view means the hook works but the product appears too late.
  5. Outreach tab: DMs, collab invites, replies, samples. A drop here shows up in revenue two to three weeks later.

2Cruva the data underneath

Wherecruva.com (shop: Upper Echelon Products)
Is this the placeYes. The content reporting tool. Every dashboard number comes from here: videos, creators, samples, live streams, ads. You rarely need to log in; the dashboard pulls it.
What to do
  1. Connections tab: API access lives here. If the dashboard goes stale, the fix starts here.
  2. Drill into a single creator or video when the dashboard flags something interesting.

3TikTok Shop Seller Center source of truth for money

Whereseller-us.tiktok.com
Is this the placeYes. Confirmed GMV, orders, units. The dashboard's "yesterday confirmed" strip reads from here (via Cruva). When they disagree, Seller Center wins.
What to do
  1. Check yesterday's strip on the dashboard, confirm it matches.
  2. GMV Max lives here. The organic vs GMV Max split comes from this console.

Wherekalodata.com

✓The weekly ritual ten minutes

  1. GMV tab: up or down week over week? That is the pacing.
  2. Financials: net after ads, commissions, refunds. Are we getting hit, and where?
  3. Products: which product carries the week. Where a slowdown lands first.
  4. Creative: top videos, hooks, revenue per view. What to adjust.
  5. Outreach: DMs, collabs, samples. What will hit revenue in 2 to 3 weeks.
  6. Sampling decision: inbound requests approve by default; outbound push confirmed against the master sheet gross profit, then greenlit.
